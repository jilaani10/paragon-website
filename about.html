About Paragon Educational Complex

Located at Abirem-Nwamaase, off the Antoa Road, in the Kwabre East District of the Ashanti Region of Ghana, Paragon Educational Complex builds strong foundations for lifelong learning from Creche to Junior High School. We combine sound academic instruction with Islamic values and Quranic memorization, so that every child grows in knowledge, character, and faith.

Our Mission & Vision

Building the foundation for tomorrow's leaders

🎯

Our Mission

To provide exceptional education that nurtures the whole child — academically, morally, and in faith. We combine the Ghana Education Service curriculum with Islamic Studies and Qur'an memorization, creating a supportive environment where every student grows in knowledge, character, and faith.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a leading Islamic educational institution recognized for academic excellence, excellence in Islamic studies, and the development of confident, well-mannered individuals who excel both in this world and the Hereafter.

💎

Our Values

Excellence, good character, integrity and respect. These core values, rooted in the teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah, guide everything we do and every decision we make for our students' future.

Our History

A journey of educational excellence

2013

Foundation

Paragon Educational Complex was founded with a vision to provide quality education from early childhood through junior high school in the Ashanti Region of Ghana.

2018

Expansion

We expanded our facilities and added modern classrooms, a library, and playground equipment to enhance the learning experience.

2023

Innovation

We integrated technology into our curriculum and launched our after-school programs to support working parents.

2024

Excellence

Today, we serve over 500 students from creche to JHS, maintaining our commitment to educational excellence and holistic development.

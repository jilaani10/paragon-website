Why Choose Paragon Educational Complex?

Excellence in education, care in every detail

Academic Excellence

Comprehensive curriculum aligned with the Ghana Education Service (GES), enriched with Islamic Studies and Quranic memorization. We build strong foundational skills while nurturing critical thinking, creativity, and good character in every student from Creche to JHS, preparing them for BECE and beyond.

Dedicated Teachers

Qualified and GES-trained teachers, alongside experienced Islamic studies instructors. Our passionate educators are committed to nurturing both academic excellence and Islamic character in every child.

Modern Facilities

Well-equipped classrooms, a library, playground, and a spacious mosque on campus for daily Salah and Islamic gatherings. A safe, welcoming environment designed to nurture both learning and worship.

Holistic Development

Beyond academics, we nurture arts, sports, and leadership, alongside Islamic manners (Akhlaq) and moral upbringing. Every student discovers and develops their unique talents while growing into a well-rounded individual, grounded in strong Islamic values.

Tech-Integrated Learning

Age-appropriate learning materials and modern teaching methods, combined with traditional Quranic instruction. Preparing students to excel in both the Ghanaian school curriculum and their Islamic education.

Individual Attention

Small class sizes ensure personalized attention for every child. Our teachers understand that each student is unique — learning and growing at their own pace, academically and in faith.

Our Academic Programs

Nurturing young minds from Creche to Junior High School

Creche

A safe, loving environment where our youngest learners explore through sensory play, Phase 1 Phonics, and gentle care routines — including basic Islamic phrases and daily du'as to nurture familiarity with their faith from the very start.

Nursery

Play-based learning following GES early years guidelines — building language, social skills, and early numeracy through storytelling, arts, and outdoor play, alongside basic Islamic manners, short surahs, and Arabic alphabet recognition.

Kindergarten

Structured, play-based preparation for Primary School in line with the GES KG curriculum — covering literacy, numeracy, creative arts, and physical development, integrated with daily Quranic recitation, Islamic studies, and Arabic language basics to build strong academic foundation.

Primary School (Grade 1–6)

Comprehensive GES Standards-Based Curriculum alongside daily Islamic Studies, Quranic recitation, and Arabic language classes to build strong academic and moral foundations.

Junior High School (Grade 7–9)

Rigorous preparation for the BECE with advanced academics across core GES subjects, alongside continued Islamic Studies and Quranic memorization, guiding students toward secondary education with strong character and career direction.

Tahfiz Program

For our boarding students: Dedicated Qur'an memorization (Hifz) sessions held daily after Asr prayers until 5:30pm, guided by qualified Huffaz. A focused, structured programme that nurtures a strong connection with the Qur'an alongside regular academic studies.

Daily Life at School

A typical day filled with learning, play, and growth

Morning Islamic Studies

The school day begins with an hour of Islamic Studies, grounding students in Qur'an, values, and character before academic lessons begin.

Secular Studies (Morning Session)

Students move into core academic lessons — English, Mathematics, Science, and more — following the Ghana Education Service curriculum, until the first break at 10:00am.

Break, Zuhr Prayer & Lunch

Classes resume after break and continue until midday. At 12:30pm, students observe Zuhr prayer, followed by a nutritious lunch.

Afternoon Studies

Secular lessons continue after lunch, wrapping up as the school day closes at 3:00pm.

Siesta, Asr Prayer & Tahfiz (Boarders)

Boarding students rest during siesta until 3:40pm, then observe Asr prayer before heading into their Tahfiz (Qur'an memorization) sessions until 5:30pm.

Evening Routine (Boarders)

After bathing and supper, boarders observe Maghrib (around 6:30pm) and Isha (around 7:30pm) prayers, followed by evening prep studies until 9:30pm before bedtime.
